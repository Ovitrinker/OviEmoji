## OviEmoji 1.0.0

First release.

* 1870 emojis with Twemoji graphics in chat and on signs
* Shortcodes like `:smile:` are sent as plain text. Players with the mod see the image, players
  without it see the shortcode.
* Emoji picker in the chat and on the sign editing screen
* Autocomplete after typing `:` and two letters
* Pasted Unicode emojis are converted to shortcodes before sending
* Minecraft 1.21.11, 26.2 and 26.3 (Fabric)
